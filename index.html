How it works

What happens between your website and AI

A customer asks: “When will order 4821 arrive?” Before the answer comes back, the question passes through eight layers. Here is what each one does.

Task

  1. 01Access

    Decides who can use the AI and what each person is allowed to see.

    Without it: One customer’s question could reach another customer’s data.

    In our example role: customer sees only their own orders

  2. 02Cache

    Reuses answers to questions it has already handled, saving time and money.

    Without it: You pay the model again for every identical question.

    In our example miss not asked before, so it moves on

  3. 03Router

    Picks the right model for each task: a fast, cheap one for simple questions and a stronger one for hard problems.

    Without it: Everything runs on one model that is either too expensive or not good enough.

    In our example fast model a simple question needs no more

  4. 04RAG Retrieval-Augmented Generation

    Finds the relevant passages in your documents and data and hands them to the model, so it answers from your facts.

    Without it: The model answers from general knowledge and may make things up.

    In our example delivery policy the document behind the answer

  5. 05Prompt

    The instructions for the model: its role, tone, rules and answer format. We version them like code.

    Without it: Answers drift over time and nobody knows why.

    In our example support v12 the current support instructions

  6. 06Tools

    Let the AI act instead of only talking: look up an order, query a database, send an email.

    Without it: The AI can tell you what to do, but it can’t do it.

    In our example orders.find(4821) looks the order up in your system

  7. 07Guardrails

    Check what goes in and what comes out: hide personal data, block forbidden actions, ask a person to approve.

    Without it: One bad answer goes straight to your customer.

    In our example personal data hidden the address stays out of the answer

  8. 08Observability

    Records every step, how long it took and what it cost, so problems can be found and quality measured.

    Without it: When the AI gets something wrong, nobody can tell why.

    In our example 1.2 s · 840 tokens time and cost of this answer
