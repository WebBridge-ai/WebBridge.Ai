How it works

What happens between your website and AI

A customer asks: “When will order 4821 arrive?” and gets an answer seconds later. Behind it is technology that can get complicated, but with us it’s simple.

Task

  1. 01Access

    Makes sure people only see what they’re allowed to see.

    Without it: One customer’s question could reach another customer’s data.

    A customer can open

    • Their own orders
    • Other customers’ orders
    • Internal price lists

  2. 02Cache

    If someone asks the same thing again, the answer is already there. That saves time and money.

    Without it: You pay the model again for every identical question.

    The same question, asked twice

    First time1.2 s
    Second time0.02 s

  3. 03Router

    A simple question goes to a fast, cheap model. A hard one goes to a stronger model.

    Without it: You either overpay for simple questions or get weak answers to hard ones.

    Each question gets the right model

    • Where is my order?fast model
    • Why did sales drop in May?stronger model

  4. 04RAG Retrieval-Augmented Generation

    Finds the right parts of your documents and data and gives them to the AI, so it answers from your facts instead of guessing.

    Without it: The model answers from general knowledge and may make things up.

    delivery-policy.pdf

    Orders with a courier arrive within 2 business days.

    used in the answer

  5. 05Prompt

    The AI’s instructions: what its job is, how it should talk and what it must never do.

    Without it: Answers drift over time and nobody knows why.

    support prompt · v12

    role:
    customer support
    tone:
    short and friendly
    rule:
    never guess a delivery date

  6. 06Tools

    So the AI can do things, not just talk: find an order, check a database, send an email.

    Without it: The AI can tell you what to do, but it can’t do it.

    The AI calls your system

    orders.find(4821)
    status:
    with courier
    arrives:
    Wednesday

  7. 07Guardrails

    The last check: hides personal data, stops what isn’t allowed and asks a person when it should.

    Without it: One bad answer goes straight to your customer.

    Checked before it reaches the customer

    Your package is going to 12 Main Street and arrives on Wednesday.

    address hidden

  8. 08Observability

    Keeps a record of what happened, how long it took and what it cost. When something goes wrong, you can see why.

    Without it: When the AI gets something wrong, nobody can tell why.

Before you write to us

Common questions

What is AI integration?

Connecting AI to your website, app or company data. Instead of generic answers, the AI works with what’s actually in your business.

Do I have to change my website or system?

Usually not. We connect AI to what you already have. If something needs adjusting, or you don’t have anything yet, we’ll build it.

Is my data safe?

We deal with security from the start: we set who can see what, hide personal data and keep a record of everything. We agree where the data runs and which model we use based on what you need, including GDPR.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the scope. Tell us what you want to solve and you’ll know the price before any work starts.

How do we start?

Write to us about what’s slowing you down. We’ll look together at whether AI makes sense for it and suggest the next step.
