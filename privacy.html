This page explains what personal data WebBridgeAI s.r.o. processes on webbridge.ai, why, and what rights you have.

Who we are

WebBridgeAI s.r.o., Súmračná 22, 821 02 Bratislava, Slovakia. For anything about your data, write to info@webbridge.ai.

Contact form

When you write to us through the form, we receive your name, email address, company (if you fill it in), your message and the language of the page.

  • Purpose: to reply to you and talk about a possible collaboration.
  • Legal basis: steps taken at your request before entering into a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). You don’t need to give consent and there is no checkbox.
  • Retention: we delete messages within 12 months, unless we need them longer because of a contract that follows.
  • Protection against spam: Cloudflare Turnstile checks that the form is used by a person. Your IP address is used to limit the number of messages and appears for a short time in the server’s technical logs.

Analytics

Only if you choose “Accept” in the cookie bar do we use Google Analytics 4 to see how the site is used: which pages people visit, where they come from and which buttons they use, including the steps of the contact form. We never send the content of the form or your contact details to Google.

  • Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR and § 109 of Act No. 452/2021 Coll. on electronic communications).
  • Settings: Google signals and ad personalisation are off. Data are kept for 14 months.
  • Provider: Google Ireland Limited. Google may transfer data to the USA under the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework.
  • Withdrawing consent: at any time under “Cookie settings” at the bottom of every page. The Google Analytics cookies are then deleted.

Who else processes the data

  • Hetzner Online GmbH (Germany): servers in the EU for this site and the form service.
  • Brevo SAS (France): delivers messages from the form to our mailbox.
  • Porkbun LLC (USA): forwards email sent to info@webbridge.ai.
  • Cloudflare, Inc. (USA): Turnstile protection against spam.
  • Google Ireland Limited: Google Analytics, only with your consent.

Transfers outside the EU rely on the safeguards GDPR requires, such as the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses.

Your rights

You can ask for access to your data, have it corrected or deleted, restrict its processing, receive it in a portable format and object to its processing. Where we rely on consent, you can withdraw it at any time. Just write to info@webbridge.ai.

You can also complain to the Office for Personal Data Protection of the Slovak Republic, Hraničná 12, 820 07 Bratislava, dataprotection.gov.sk.
